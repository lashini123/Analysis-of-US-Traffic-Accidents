## 1. Dataset Description

This project uses official traffic crash data from the City of Chicago containing detailed records of road accidents including time, location conditions, environmental factors, road characteristics, and injury outcomes.

Each record represents a **single traffic crash event**.

The dataset includes both **numerical and categorical variables**, making it suitable for trend analysis, severity studies, and safety-related insight generation.

---

## 2. Analytical Objectives

The primary objective of this analysis is to identify patterns and risk factors associated with traffic crashes in order to support evidence-based safety recommendations.

---

## 3. Business / Policy Questions

The analysis focuses on answering the following questions:

### Time-Based Trends

* How do crash volumes change across years and months?
* Are there seasonal patterns in accident occurrences?

### Environmental Impact

* How do weather and lighting conditions affect crash frequency?
* Are certain conditions associated with higher injury severity?

### Road and Traffic Characteristics

* How does posted speed limit relate to injury outcomes?
* Which road and traffic control conditions are most risky?

### Injury Severity

* What proportion of crashes result in injuries or fatalities?
* Which factors contribute most to severe crash outcomes?

---

## 4. Granularity of Data

Each observation represents a **single crash event**, recorded at the time of occurrence.
This allows the analysis to be conducted at:

* Event level
* Monthly aggregated level
* Yearly aggregated level
